Actionable Treatment Plan

Agronomic Guidance & Intervention
⚡ Immediate Action

Prune infected lower foliage immediately and dispose away from the field. Sanitize cutting tools with alcohol solution.

Recommended Category: Copper-based Fungicide / Bio-Fungicide
Organic Option: Trichoderma viride @ 5g/liter
🚿 Application Guidance

Ensure uniform spray coverage on lower leaf undersides using a fine hollow-cone nozzle. Apply early morning when dew has dried.

🛡️ Preventive Measures
  • Adopt drip irrigation to avoid wet foliage
  • Maintain proper plant spacing (45-60cm) for airflow
  • Rotate crop with non-solanaceous crops next season
❌ Things to Avoid: Do NOT overhead irrigate during active outbreak. Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizers which promote soft susceptible leaves.
